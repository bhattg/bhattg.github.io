Gantavya Bhatt

PhD candidate · University of Washington, Seattle

I am a final year PhD candidate at the University of Washington, Seattle, where I am working with Prof. Jeffery A. Bilmes. I like to work on improving the data efficiency of large-scale models for both training and inference using submodular optimization. In Summer'25, I interned at NVIDIA - Applied Deep Learning Research, working with Mohammad Shoeybi and Mostafa Patwary. In summer '24, I interned at NVIDIA's Applied Deep Learning Research Team with Rafael Valle and Kevin Shih.

I am on the Industry Job Market. If you think I can be a good fit for your research roles, please don't hesitate to reach out!

Before joining the PhD program, I completed my undergraduate degree at IIT Delhi, in Electrical Engineering, where I worked with Dr Sumeet Agarwal and Dr Prathosh AP. I'm also an avid photographer (Checkout my Unsplash) and hiker! When not working, I can be found messing around with my camera, Google Earth, or hiking in North Cascades National Park.
