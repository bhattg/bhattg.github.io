The photographs I shared in 2025 bring me back to the changing light of the Pacific Northwest: warm colors on Mount Blum, a moonrise above Mount Shuksan, and layers of mountains fading into the distance. The collection moves from the last fall colors around Mount Baker to snow-covered peaks, forests softened by fog, and winter sunsets over the Olympic Peninsula.

The city offers its own kind of landscape. Mount Rainier rises beyond Seattle, the Golden Gate Bridge frames San Francisco, and Griffith Observatory glows against Los Angeles at night. I’ve brought those contrasts together here: mountain silhouettes and city skylines, quiet forests and busy streets, soft winter light and the colors of sunset.
