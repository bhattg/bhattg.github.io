Looking back through the photographs I shared in 2024, the collection moves between two very different worlds: the quiet of the mountains and the energy of the city. The Cascades appear under snow, sunset colors, and northern lights; farther south, desert rock formations take on a different character beneath the Milky Way.

There are quieter details here, too—a road through the redwoods, reflections below Mount Stuart, and a wolf in Yellowstone’s Lamar Valley. Seattle, New York, and Las Vegas bring a change of pace. Together, these frames bring mountain light, open landscapes, and city scenes into one collection. Here are some of the highlights.
