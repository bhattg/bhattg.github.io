Outside research, photography gives me a reason to slow down and look a little longer. I’m drawn to mountain ridgelines, night skies, and the changing light of familiar places. The Pacific Northwest runs through much of this collection—from the Cascades and Mount Rainier to Seattle’s skyline and the forests around them.

The view changes beyond the Northwest: desert formations beneath the Milky Way, redwood roads, and city scenes from New York to San Francisco. This is a small selection of those subjects and places. Explore a few frames below, or follow the yearly highlights for a longer look.
